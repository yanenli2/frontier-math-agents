# Changelog

## [0.1.0.0] - 2026-09-27

### Added
- A faithful Lean-checked proof that every positive multiple of four is a sum of two positive integers whose Liouville values are both −1, with equal summands allowed.
- The natural-language proof and an offline HTML edition with all 401 mathematical expressions typeset.
- Pinned Lean and Mathlib configuration, statement snapshots, source provenance, independent reviews, and build/axiom evidence for reproducing the result under the July 31, 2026 source cutoff.
- Git exclusions for rebuildable caches, local team communications, recovery snapshots, and the downloaded third-party paper PDF.

The original conjecture for every even integer greater than two remains unresolved; this release proves its multiples-of-four case.
