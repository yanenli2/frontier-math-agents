On a Goldbach-type problem for the Liouville function
Alexander P. Mangerel
Let 
λ
 denote the Liouville function. We show that for all sufficiently large integers 
N
, the (non-trivial) convolution sum bound
∣
∣
∣
∣
∑
1≤n<N
λ(n)λ(N−n)
∣
∣
∣
∣
<N−1
holds. This (essentially) answers a question posed at the 2018 AIM workshop on Sarnak's conjecture.
