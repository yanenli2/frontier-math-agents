OTHER FOCUSED ROUTES AND THEIR LIMITATIONS

A. The all-8m theorem alone does not handle 4p for odd primes p: 8 does not divide
4p. Substituting m/2 into that theorem would lose the odd multipliers.

B. The diagonal cannot solve the negative-sign residual: lambda(2m)=-lambda m=1,
so the pair (2m,2m) has the wrong sign when lambda m=-1. This is an obstruction
to that witness choice, not to all representations.

C. More fixed two-sign seeds do not by themselves cover the odd-prime core.
For any fixed finite set of seed values, choose an odd prime p larger than all
of them. A seed used purely by integer scaling to 4p must divide 4p. Its only
possible values below p are 1,2,4. None is a two-sign seed: 1 has no positive
split; 2 has only 1+1 (positive signs); 4 has splits 1+3,2+2,3+1 and no
positive-positive split. Thus finite fixed seed expansion cannot be the entire
uniform proof along that restricted strategy. This is a route assessment,
not a refutation of the requested theorem or all other constructions.

D. The accepted counting identity and count-existence equivalence specialize to
N=4m. They still require the strict bound

  2 * L (4 * m - 1) - (((4 * m : ℕ) : ℤ) - 1) < C (4 * m).

Natural subtraction inside L remains guarded by m>0; scalar subtraction is in
integers. The identity alone gives a nonnegative natural count, not positivity.
No new uniform signed/correlation estimate has been obtained here.

E. target_iff_prime_product_core is a proved equivalence, not a proof of either
side. Invoking its core family at primes 2,p without first proving Target would
be circular and was not done.
